About Coffeebreak Musician Bookings

Coffeebreak Musician Bookings is for music teachers, studios, rehearsal rooms, bands and solo musicians. Book lessons and studio time with live availability, request a gig with an instant quote, and keep a smart waitlist. Musicians get a day dashboard, gig requests and a coffee order queue. Everything runs in your web browser and is saved on your own device.

Coffee cart: order a coffee, see your cart total and press Buy it now. Pay by PayID (Teachers Mutual Bank): darkokaz@gmx.com, or pay at the counter. This app takes no card payments.
